5.2 Payment Methods

5.2.1 Available Payment Options

We offer two payment methods for HOA dues and assessments:

ACH/Bank Account Payments:

  • Direct debit from your bank account
  • Processing fee: 0.8% of transaction amount, capped at $5.00
  • Typically takes 3-5 business days to process
  • Lower cost option for residents

Credit/Debit Card Payments:

  • Immediate payment processing

  • Higher cost due to card network fees
  • Provides instant payment confirmation
